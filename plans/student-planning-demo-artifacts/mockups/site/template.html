Student Planner Mock-ups

Five screens from the UX review, drawn as static pages with Priya's real week: Biology and Chemistry lectures, cafe shifts Tue and Thu 17:00-22:00, the Biology quiz on Thursday and the lab report on Friday. The structure is the app you have today, with board tabs and widget panels. What changes is the type, the surfaces and a few interactions that tell the story better on stage.

Shared across every screen

Type on four steps: 12 meta, 14 body, 16 item titles, 20 headings, with no 10-11px text and almost no uppercase mono. Widgets get a 20px radius, no header divider and a warmer paper ground. Times are 24-hour everywhere. Each course has a tint, an ink and a stripe colour.

Block language on the calendar

Class: solid course tint. Shift: soft neutral with a cup icon. Study time: light tint with a solid left bar and a grab handle. Ghost draft: hatched with a dashed outline. Free time: a dotted gap with "2h free", only while you are planning.

Study block popover, then landing on the moment

P1-5 lead with Go to momentP1-8 show which automation made itP2-5 collapsed summary, gutter marks
  • The popover leads with where the block came from: "From Mitochondrial DNA · 1:18", the quoted line and "You marked this confusing". The primary button is "Go to moment". "Mark done" and "Edit time" come next. The date and time pickers move behind "Edit time".
