Student Planner UX Review

The demo works end to end and much of the copy is already kind and honest. What holds it back is a light-mode colour bug that hides the best moment, type that is too small to feel calm, and a handful of layout collisions on stage. Fix the P0 list and the demo will read as intentional; the P1 and P2 lists are what make it feel built for students.

If you only do five things
  1. Add light-theme overrides for the amber palette, so "Review planned" and "% sure" are readable (P0-1).
  2. Make the confused-to-calendar ripple visible: scroll to and pulse the new block, link the chip (P0-2).
  3. Make the capacity line agree with Auto-fill by saying the 2h-a-day cap out loud (P0-4).
  4. Show a "Transcribing..." state instead of an empty transcript (P0-6).
  5. Raise the type floor from 10-11px to 12-13px and fix muted-text contrast (P1-1, P1-2).
How I tested

Walked every step of the section 2 script in headless Chromium with the clock pinned, then explored as a first-time student. I built "My week" by clicking, ran Help me plan and Auto-fill, scoped to Biology, uploaded the demo lecture, marked the maternal-inheritance moment as confusing, edited the automation and tried both encores. I seeded the missed blocks for re-fit by hand because the demo seed has none.

P0 · before the demo · each one is small

Things the audience will notice

P0-1

Amber text disappears in light mode

Effort SContrast
What
The light theme inverts the neutral and emerald scales in apps/web/src/index.css but leaves amber alone. text-amber-200/300/400 (8 files: Transcript, To-do, Re-fit banner, Weakest Topics, Your Progress, All Courses, Coming up, WorkflowCard) were picked for a dark background and render at 1.2:1 on light panels. Add light values for --color-amber-200..400 (about oklch(0.5 0.12 70)), or better, swap them for a semantic --color-attention token.
Why
It hides "Review planned...", "5 moments to review", "Still to do from yesterday" and every "% sure" score. The same strings read at 15:1 in dark mode, so this is a token bug, not a design choice.
P0-2

Let the audience see the ripple

Effort SDelightDemo step 4
What
When you tap "confused", the to-do, toast and calendar block all appear, but you can't see any of it happen. (1) Fix the chip contrast (P0-1). (2) Make the chip a button that opens the block, and use 24-hour times like the calendar: "Review planned · Wed 21:30 · 30 min". (3) Add a "See it" action to the "Review added to your plan" toast. (4) When a block appears or moves, scroll the calendar to it and give it one soft 600ms glow (respect reduced motion).
Why
This is the moment that sells the product, and right now it plays out as a pale chip and a block that teleports to the 21:30 row at the very bottom of the grid, half under the frame. The demo script promises "slots itself into Wednesday evening". The data does that, but the audience can't see it.
P0-3

Keep Test Me off the demo boards, or fix its clipping

Effort SPolish
What
On Evening Review and Exam Prep, the answer field and the "Reveal Answer" button are cut in half by the widget frame. The plan already says to keep Test Me off demo boards. Either drop it from the seeded boards, or give the body a scroll container and a minimum height.
Why
It is the first thing the eye lands on when you switch boards, and clipped controls look broken.
P0-4

The capacity line contradicts Auto-fill

Effort SCopyDemo step 2
What
Help me plan says "About 12 hours of study time left this week. Your to-dos need 12." with a full bar. Then Auto-fill says "7h 30m didn't fit". The reason (a 2h-a-day cap) only appears in small print further down. Bring the cap into the headline: "About 12 free hours, up to 2h a day. Lab report write-up (7h 30m) won't fit in one go." Then offer "Split across days" before "Shrink to 2h". A student would expect splitting a long write-up, not shrinking it.
Why
Principle 6 ("honest capacity note") only works if the numbers agree. With today's seed at 10:30 Tuesday you also don't get the scripted "About 9 free hours. This needs 12." line, so retune the seed or update the runbook line.
P0-5

Click-added widgets land on top of others

Effort S (demo) / M (fix)LayoutDemo step 5
What
On a board that already has Calendar, To-do and Coming up, "Add Transcript" places the new widget over the calendar, and it then blocks clicks on calendar blocks. If no free area fits, place the widget below the lowest one and scroll to it. For the demo, add widgets before the board gets full, or press "Tidy up widgets" straight after.
Why
Step 5 is "add or remove a widget with a click". An overlap there undercuts the malleability pitch.
P0-6

An empty transcript while the lecture processes

Effort SEmpty stateDemo step 4
What
After you press Upload, the Transcript shows the title, "0 segments" and a large blank area for several seconds. The only sign of progress is "1 running" in the top bar. Show a skeleton with "Transcribing Mitochondrial DNA... this takes about a minute", then "Writing your summary..." once the transcript is in.
Why
Silence on stage reads as a crash, especially with live Whisper, where the wait is real.
P1 · softness, clarity and access

Make it feel calm and look intentional

P1-1

A real type scale, with a 12px floor

Effort MSoftness
What
Widgets use text-[10px] 88 times and text-[11px] 67 times. Uppercase mono micro-labels ("4 TO DO", "HOW YOUR COURSES COMPARE", "DUE") are everywhere. Define four steps as tokens (12 meta, 14 body, 16 item title, 20 widget heading), use them everywhere, and save uppercase mono for at most one label per widget. Use proportional numerals for times in lists; keep mono for transcript timestamps only.
Why
Tiny grey caps are the main reason the UI feels like an admin tool. Bigger, friendlier type at the same density is the cheapest way to make it feel softer.
P1-2

Muted text fails contrast in both themes

Effort SAccessibility
What
--color-text-muted is 3.1:1 on light panels and 2.5:1 on dark ones. It carries real information: due dates, "+2 in other courses", provenance, durations. Move it to about oklch(0.52 ...) in light and oklch(0.62 ...) in dark to reach 4.5:1. --color-text-secondary is fine at 7.2:1.
Why
Principle 4 ("never hide silently") depends on "+N in other courses" being readable. Today it is the faintest text on the screen.
P1-3

Help me plan squeezes the calendar

Effort MLayout
What
The planning panel takes about 300px from a 900px widget, so every block truncates and free time floods each column in pale blue. Options: let the panel grow the widget temporarily, or overlay the panel on the calendar's right edge. Show free time as a thin stripe or a dotted outline inside each gap, not a full-column wash.
Why
Step 2 is the point where Ed narrates the plan. Truncated titles and a blue wash make it hard to see what goes where.
P1-4

Tell blocks apart at a glance

Effort SColourAffordance
What
Today accepted study blocks have a dashed border that looks almost like a ghost. Shifts are heavy grey slabs, and missed blocks look like any other past block. Suggested language: class uses a solid course tint; commitment (shift) uses a soft neutral tint with a small icon, never grey-on-grey; study time uses a light course tint, a solid left bar and a grab handle on hover; ghost uses hatching with a dashed outline; missed is faded with a gentle "didn't happen" marker.
Why
Principle 5 ("commitments stay fixed; study time can move") should be visible on the grid itself, not only in the popover copy.
P1-5

Clicking a review block should lead with "Go to the moment"

Effort SAffordance
What
Clicking a study block with provenance opens a full edit form (native date and time pickers, course select, Save), with the source link tucked near the bottom. Lead with the source: the title, "From Mitochondrial DNA · 1:18", a primary "Go to moment" button, then "Mark done" and "Edit time". On landing, offset the scroll so the moment isn't hidden under the sticky summary, and flash it once.
Why
In the demo the click-through is the last beat of step 4. On a board without Transcript it currently ends on "This board has no Transcript."
P1-6

A first run that suggests where to start

Effort S/MEmpty state
What
The empty board shows a grey icon, one line and "Add widgets". Keep that, but add three one-click starters below it, named by intent: "Plan my time" (Calendar, To-do, Coming up), "Capture a lecture" (Audio Capture, Transcript), "Find what to revise" (Weakest Topics, Your Progress). Each one just adds widgets, which the student can then rearrange. It doesn't create a fixed workflow.
Why
It keeps principle 1 (tools, not workflows) while teaching what widgets are for. It also gives Ed a one-click fallback on stage.
P1-7

Phone width: switch to Day view and show the tabs scroll

Effort MResponsive
What
At 400px the week grid collapses: day labels collide ("26TUE 27WED 28THU") and blocks shrink to one letter. Default to Day view below about 640px, with prev/next to step days. The board tabs are clipped with no sign that they scroll ("ecture", "Ex"), so add an edge fade and scroll the active tab into view.
Why
Mobile-first is out of scope, but students will open this on a phone, and a broken grid is worse than a simpler view.
P1-8

Small affordance fixes

Effort S eachAffordanceAccessibility
Upload
The upload form uses placeholder-only fields (title, course). Give them visible labels: "Lecture title" and "Course".
Create
Clicking empty time defaults to "Class / commitment". A student clicking an empty slot is usually adding study time, so make that the default and show a placeholder block on the grid where they clicked.
Tick
Ticking a to-do makes it vanish with no acknowledgement. Strike it through, fade it out over about 400ms and show "Done · Undo".
Find
The automation that creates reviews lives on the Transcript widget. On the Calendar, the automations dialog says "No automations for this widget yet", so a student wondering "why is this here?" never finds it. Link the review block and chip to "Made by: When I mark something confusing... Edit".
Focus
Focus rings are visible and consistent (good). Keep them as the type changes, and check the calendar blocks, which use a 2px ring on a 2px selection ring.
P2 · warmth and personality

Make it feel like it is on the student's side

P2-1

A gentle "today" line on every board

Effort MWarmth
What
Next to the "Showing" chip, add a quiet sentence built from plan items: "Tuesday · Chemistry at 14:00 · shift 17:00-22:00 · 2h free". It is a view of the same data, not a new workflow.
Why
It turns the top bar from tool chrome into a friend who knows your week, and it reinforces principle 2 (the data is the thing).
P2-2

Softer surfaces and course colour with character

Effort MSoftnessColour
What
Every widget has the same 16px radius, hairline border and header strip, so nothing stands out. Drop the header divider, add 4px more inner padding, and use a softer 20px radius on widgets with 10px on inner items. Give each course a fuller, paired palette (tint, ink, stripe) and use it on the widget icon when the board is scoped. In the board menu, colour each group's icons (Capture, Study, Plan, Review) so they are easy to scan.
Why
Courses are how students think. Colour that carries meaning feels warm; uniform grey feels clinical.
P2-3

Schedule reviews at humane times

Effort MCalm
What
Every automatic placement I saw landed in the last free slot of the day (Fri 21:30, then Wed 21:30) or at the current minute (Today 10:30). Prefer the earliest slot that starts 30+ minutes from now and ends before a "wind down" time (default 21:00, a layer-2 setting), and leave a 15-minute buffer after classes.
Why
"Calm, not guilt" includes not booking a confusing topic at 21:30 after a five-hour shift. Reviewing sooner also helps memory.
P2-4

Choreograph one signature motion

Effort MMotionDelight
What
Build one orchestrated sequence, not effects everywhere. The confused flag fills, a small card lifts off the moment, the To-do row slides in, and the calendar block grows into its slot with the same tint. When the automation changes, the block glides to its new time instead of jumping. Ghost blocks fade in one after another on Auto-fill. Everything else stays still. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
Why
"Composable widgets over shared data" is abstract. Seeing one fact move across three widgets makes it concrete.
P2-5

Summary and transcript as study material

Effort S/MReadability
What
The summary is three 12px lines; the third is cut off with "...". Give it full sentences at body size, and collapse it to one line once the student scrolls instead of keeping it sticky. Moments that carry tags could have a coloured gutter mark so confusing passages are easy to spot when scrolling.
Why
The transcript is where students spend the most reading time.
P2-6

Re-fit and Activity in student language

Effort SCopy
What
Re-fit is kind ("1 block didn't happen. Re-fit it?"), but on a Biology-scoped board it counts only the Biology block while the Chemistry one sits dimmed on the calendar. Say "+1 in other courses" there too. The Activity panel lists job names ("Write a transcript", "0s"). Write outcomes instead: "Transcript ready · Mitochondrial DNA · just now", each linked to its result.
Why
Principle 4 applies to banners too, and Activity is where a curious student checks what the app did for them.

Working well · protect these in any redesign

Keep these

The automation sentence. "When I mark something confusing, add a 30-min review by tomorrow." It has inline selects, says "Saved. Moved 1 review to fit." and explains its scope. This is the best screen in the app.
Honest Auto-fill copy. "Nothing is saved until you accept", "Keep my plan" and "Lab report write-up is due soonest, so it gets first pick" all set the right tone.
Scope you can see. "Showing: Biology", "Biology highlighted" and dimmed (not hidden) other courses all follow principles 3 and 4.
Click-to-add with the drawer staying open. The board menu is grouped by intent (Capture, Study, Plan, Review), and each tile has a short description.
Visible focus rings and labelled icon buttons. Every top-bar icon has a name ("Tidy up widgets", "Board appearance", "Switch to dark mode").
Calm re-fit. "1 block didn't happen. Re-fit it?" with no red and no overdue pile.
